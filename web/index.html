    System size and rates

    Hours like 8-11,18-21 mean from that hour until the next. 8-11 is 8:00 to 11:00. Efficiency is one way: charge stores power times this number, and discharge takes power divided by it. Charge now is applied only if you edit that box. The bill is an estimate at your rates, not a utility invoice.
