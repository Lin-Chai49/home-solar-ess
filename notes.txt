Home solar storage (local sim)

Python 3.9+. No extra packages. Full description is in README.md.

    cd this folder
    python3 server.py

Open http://127.0.0.1:8765

Default is Auto: a short solar/load forecast plus time-of-use rates pick charge or discharge.
Large cell spread switches to active balance. You can still use self-use, peak/off-peak, or manual.

Size and rates are at the bottom of the page. The bill is an estimate at your rates, not a utility invoice.

Cells are a simulated 16s LFP pack. Charge is limited by the highest cell, discharge by the lowest.
Cell 7 is weaker on purpose. Cloud cover is a slow random factor, not a weather forecast.
There is no thermal-runaway probability.

LLM (optional):
    Copy llm.example.json to data/llm.json, set enabled true, fill api_key and model.
    Or use ESS_LLM_KEY, ESS_LLM_URL, ESS_LLM_MODEL.
    OpenAI /v1/chat/completions. The model only suggests (p_kw / why / balance).
    Power still passes SOC limits and cell protection. No request without a key.
    Status: GET http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/llm
    Ask once: POST the same URL.

Check the sim:

    python3 server.py check
